Start with the help pages built into the app. If a help page doesn't solve it, you can email Waycar support for one-on-one help or ask the r/waycar community on Reddit. Both are in the app under **Settings** › **Support**, and on the [support page](/support).

## Read help in the app

- **From Settings:** open **Settings**, then under **Support** tap **Help**. Search for a word or browse by topic.
- **From a feature screen:** tap **Learn More** (the question mark) to open the help page for that screen.
- **With a keyboard on iPad:** press ⌘?, then choose **Waycar Help**. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/keyboard-shortcuts#open-help).
- **Ask an agent:** on a help page, tap **Ask an Agent** to open the New Agent sheet with that page attached. Review the Host, folder, agent and name, then tap **Start**. The agent reads the page and helps you with it on your Host. The button appears once you have a Host. It's the speech bubble with a plus at the top of the screen. The first 10 times you open a help page on a device, a labeled **Ask an Agent** button also shows above the page text.

## Contact support from the app

1. Open **Settings**. See [Open Settings](/help/settings#open-settings).
2. Under **Support**, tap one of:
   - **Email Support** to write to support@waycar.dev in your mail app. Every message gets an email answer.
   - **Community on Reddit** to open r/waycar for discussion, bugs and feature requests.

## What to include

- Your iPhone or iPad model and its iOS version.
- Your Waycar version and build, from **Settings** › **About** › **Version**.
- What you see on screen, word for word if there's a message. A screenshot helps.
- Whether the Host uses herdr or tmux, and whether it's a Mac or Linux.

Never send passwords, private keys, tokens or other secrets.

## Find your Waycar version

Open **Settings** and scroll to **About**. **Version** shows the app version, then the build number in parentheses. See [Settings at a glance](/help/settings#about).
