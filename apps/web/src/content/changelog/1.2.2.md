### Added

- **Read an agent's conversation with no signal**: when a terminal can't
  connect, a card appears over it that says what is wrong ("You're offline"
  when your device has no network; with one, the Host's own status such as
  "Host Unavailable", "Can't reach your Host" or "Terminal
  disconnected") with **Read saved copy** and how old the copy is.
  A session that is just slow to load on a working network shows the card
  only after about eight seconds. Tapping it opens the
  conversation Waycar saved on this device, as text you can scroll, under a
  banner that says it's a saved copy, with the
  Composer under it so you can write a reply that goes out once you're back
  online. Waycar saves the conversation of agents you open, and keeps
  the eight agents that finished most recently up to date while the app is
  open. An agent gets a saved copy once it reports its session to Waycar
  (on herdr most agents only with herdr's integration installed, on tmux
  with the Status Hooks) and the Host's Waycar plugin can read its
  conversation.

### Changed

- **A dropped terminal connection no longer covers the screen**: a status dot
  in the toolbar shows it, you can keep reading, and tapping the dot offers
  Reattach. Waycar also reattaches once on its own before it shows the
  terminal as detached.
