### Changed

- **Waycar Pro**: pick a plan that fits. Pro is now $5 a month or $40 a
  year, and both lift the free limit of 10 Launches in any 30 days. The
  paywall and the lock card on a hidden Agent show both plans. You can
  switch plans in Manage Subscription; the switch starts at your next
  renewal. (#42)

Since 1.3.0, Waycar is free: every feature, with no Launch limit.

- **No Host yet**: the Console and Hosts list the exact setup steps (install
  and open herdr, turn on SSH, run the one Waycar install command, which
  shows a Pairing Code), with Scan to Pair first and Add Manually under it.
  Tap the command to copy it; a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account can
  paste it. On iPad the steps fill the space beside the list. After you add
  your first Host, its page opens in Hosts. (#44)

### Fixed

- With one Host or none, the Console's bottom bar stays at the bottom of the
  screen instead of floating halfway up. (#44)
